## 🚀 Expertise

Passionate about crafting seamless mobile and web experiences, I specialize in **Flutter** for mobile development and **Laravel** for backend systems. My journey involves a mix of academic rigor and hands-on project building, where I've developed applications like **ScanGo**, a ticket management solution, and **Gajiku**, a payroll management system.

Beyond coding, I've had the unique opportunity to serve as a volunteer teacher at **Sekolah Indonesia Riyadh** in Saudi Arabia, which honed my communication and leadership skills. I also have a keen eye for visual aesthetics and photography, which I often blend into my UI/UX design process.

Currently, I am expanding my horizons by diving into the world of **Cloud Computing**—specifically preparing for the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner** certification—and exploring the integration of **Generative AI** into my development workflow.
